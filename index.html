06 · METHOD & LIMITS

Designed to be questioned.

The strongest report is not the one with the most confident story. It is the one where readers can distinguish observation, derived metric, AI-assisted classification and interpretation.

Evidence pipeline

  1. Acquire public agenda and sponsor records.
  2. Preserve raw values and source URLs.
  3. Normalize records and resolve display-name aliases conservatively.
  4. Classify topics with a stable taxonomy and recorded confidence.
  5. Derive metrics from approved records.
  6. Audit sources, schema, statistics, privacy and narrative claims.

Interpretation boundary

Agenda composition is used as a proxy for programmed community attention. It does not directly measure attendance, audience voting, labour-market demand, technical expertise, sponsorship spend, company hiring, or causal impact.

A visible rise is evidence that a topic occupied more of the collected agenda—not proof that the entire Perth technology market adopted it.
Methodology note: This report measures the composition of published agendas and the frequency of recorded appearances. It does not directly measure speaker ability, corporate spending, the size of Perth’s talent pool, or labour-market demand. AI is primarily used for classification and validation; inferred fields should retain supporting evidence, confidence levels, and review status.
